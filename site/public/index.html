What it does

Any camera

The built-in FaceTime camera, USB webcams and capture cards, your iPhone, and virtual cameras like OBS, NDI and Iriun. Plug one in and it appears in the menu.

Any sound input

Every microphone and audio interface the Mac sees, or no sound. A hairline meter on the left edge shows the level in dB, with a peak that holds.

One key records

The round key turns red and the take goes to Movies/mb_date_time.mov: H.265, 1920×1080, 10 Mb/s, AAC sound.

Time code from the clock

While it records, REC 14:03:27:12 runs at the bottom. The file carries a real time code track starting at the time of day of its first frame, ready for multicam sync in DaVinci Resolve.

Nothing but the picture

The controls lie half see-through over the picture, so nothing is cropped. ⌘F is full screen with only the image; Esc brings the controls back.

Written to disk every second

A take is saved in one-second pieces while it records, so a crash costs a second, not the recording.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded WebcamRecorder.dmg and drag Webcam Recorder (Mantra) onto Applications.
  2. Open it. The first time, macOS says it cannot check the app: it is signed by its author, not through Apple's paid program. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and press Open Anyway.
  3. Allow the camera and the microphone when asked. If you said no, the amber PERMISSIONS at the top right opens the switch.
